1. Documented Tokenomics & Utility: The Reflexive Yield Engine

At the core of $KNOTS is an on-chain transfer tax mechanism designed to reward long-term community participation. With a fixed total supply of 1,000,000,000 $KNOTS (1 Billion tokens minted under contract address 8RVBk8vxLiUHueLUW1f4izFVqN3nWippLhkohKg6EGkS), the protocol enforces specific economic rules:

  • 3% Transfer Tax: Assessed on every transaction of $KNOTS across decentralized exchanges and wallets.
  • Pro-Rata $STONK Payouts: Rather than distributing rewards in the native $KNOTS token—which often triggers continuous sell pressure—accumulated taxes are converted and distributed pro-rata to holders directly in $STONK.
  • Operational Deductions: An approximate 2.5% fee is deducted from collected taxes to cover Solana transaction gas and operational network overhead for automated batched distributions.
  • Minimum Balance Threshold: To optimize distribution efficiency, payouts are triggered once accumulated taxes reach viable thresholds and are credited to wallets maintaining a minimum balance of $20 in $KNOTS at observation.
Key Utility Insight: Unlike inflationary staking tokens that dilute circulating holders, the transfer tax directly captures external trading turnover, creating positive-sum passive accrual denominated in $STONK.
